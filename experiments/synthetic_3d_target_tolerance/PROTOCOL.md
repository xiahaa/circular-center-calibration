# Figure 7 protocol boundary

The paper publishes the radius-normalized deformation sweep and vector figure,
but not the generator, trial count, or robust-method thresholds. A 64-point,
180-degree, `sigma=0.005r` protocol with a quadratic normal warp and symmetric
ellipse axes recovers the published direct-CGA curve. Those choices and the
vector-decoded reference values are explicit in `protocol.yaml`; differences
for the robust methods remain measurable in `paper_comparison.csv`.
